Welcome to *Fons Voyage* — the new lab blog of the **Goff Lab** at the Johns
Hopkins School of Medicine.

We're starting this site as a place to share the in-between work that
doesn't fit in a paper: half-finished analyses, methods notes, weird
single-cell observations, conference recaps, and the occasional
cephalopod sighting.

Expect posts on:

- single-cell and spatial transcriptomics of the developing brain
- the computational scaffolding we build to make sense of it all
- comparative neurobiology, with a soft spot for invertebrates
- whatever else seems worth writing down

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) is the calmest way
to do it. Otherwise, the [archive](/archive.html) and
[tags](/categories/index.html) pages will fill out as we go.

Thanks for coming aboard.
